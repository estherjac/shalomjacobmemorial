Life & Legacy

איש הספר

Rav Shalom זצ״ל was an איש הספר, and his untimely passing left a void in the Torah world — and especially in the world of Sefarim.

A partner at a world-class law firm and a Talmid Chacham of significant note, he spent decades amassing an unparalleled collection of more than 50,000 Sefarim, and brought more than 25 forgotten works of Rishonim and Achronim back into print.
