Continuing His Work

The Publishing Project

Bringing the words of Rishonim and Achronim back to life

The Publishing Project continues the work Rav Shalom זצ״ל carried out in his brief lifetime — publishing and revitalizing the teachings of Rishonim and Achronim, some never published and others long ago disappeared from circulation.

Endorsed by Gedolei Torah

Each Sefer received Haskamos from esteemed Rabbanim and Roshei Yeshiva. Letters arrived from around the world expressing gratitude for these publications — often including Chidushim on the Sefarim themselves. Select a Rav to read the Haskamah.

Works in Progress

The work he left unfinished

At the time of his passing, Rav Shalom זצ״ל was passionately working on several Sefarim, some near completion. He had also co-founded a Machon dedicated to printing the unpublished manuscripts of Rav Zvi Hirsch Grodzinsky זצ״ל — with a goal of publishing some 50 of them, alongside precious older Sefarim long out of circulation.
