Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

The Shalom Jacob Memorial Institute respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you choose to send. We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we use it only to respond to you and to keep you informed about the Institute’s work if you ask us to.

Donations

Donations are processed by a third-party payment provider. We do not store your card or bank details on this website.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, email us at shalomjacobmemorial@gmail.com.
