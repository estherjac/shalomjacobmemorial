Join us in continuing a legacy of Torah scholarship

In his lifetime, Rav Shalom זצ״ל single-handedly brought dozens of forgotten Torah texts back to life — from meticulous research, study and writing to ensuring the highest quality of printing. It was his passion and life’s mission, one that left him precious little time to sleep.

Now the responsibility to carry this mission forward falls upon us, and we need your help.
