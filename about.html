Life & Legacy

Rav Shalom Jacob זצ״ל — Talmid Chacham, איש הספר, and devoted servant of Torah.

Life & Legacy

An איש הספר

Rav Shalom זצ״ל was an איש הספר, and his untimely passing left a void in the Torah world — and especially in the world of Sefarim.

Over decades, he amassed an unparalleled private Sefarim collection, and his expertise and knowledge about Sefarim was unrivaled. A successful partner at Locke Lord LLP, he used the earnings from his law practice as a means of financing his study of Torah and the acquisition and publication of Sefarim.

הוי ממעט בעסק ועסוק בתורה
“Minimize your work and dedicate yourself to Torah” — a dictum of Chazal he personified.
01 — Influences

Shaped by Gedolim

His knowledge of Sefarim and Torah was shaped by Torah luminaries such as Rav Tuvia Goldstein זצ״ל, Rav Chaim Yitzchok Poupko זצ״ל (a Ben Bayis of the Chafetz Chaim), Rav Yitzchak Koppelman זצ״ל of Lucerne, Rav Chaim Kaufman זצ״ל of Gateshead and Rav Shmuel Akiva Schlesinger שליט״א of Strasbourg.

02 — Sefarim

A Trove Without Equal

He spent decades amassing more than 50,000 volumes, many rare, spanning the space and time of Jewish history. He knew each Sefer deeply — its author, content and history — and could easily find any Sefer in his vast library.

03 — Learning

Hasmada Above All

A devoted Talmid of Rav Tuvia Goldstein and Rav Chaim Yitzchok Poupko for decades, and early on of Rav Hillel David שליט״א. During his illness, when he could no longer write his Chidushim with his right hand, he learned to write with his left.

04 — Shiurim

Always Ready to Help

For more than two decades he faithfully attended Rav Yisroel Dovid Schlesinger’s Shiurim. Rav Schlesinger depended on him to provide rare Sefarim, and whenever called upon he acted with speed, devotion and enthusiasm.

05 — Publications

Reviving Forgotten Torah

He republished more than 25 Sefarim — some from manuscript — on topics from the laws of Yayin Nesach to Mefarshim on the Haggadah, each enriched with his own commentary, annotations and biographies of their authors.

06 — Vasikin

Never Missing a Day

He davened Vasikin daily — Shabbos, Yom Tov, Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur — rain or shine, even when traveling for business. Each Erev Shabbos he made calls to ensure a Minyan, often at his own expense.
