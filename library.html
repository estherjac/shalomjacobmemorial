The Library Project

Safeguarding 50,000 Sefarim for future generations

The Shalom Jacob Memorial Library Project is dedicated to preserving the legacy of Rav Shalom זצ״ל by organizing, archiving and digitizing his unparalleled and historic collection of ~50,000 Sefarim — and building an appropriate home in which to house them.

  • Organize & archive a collection built over some 45 years
  • Digitize the Sefarim so they remain accessible to Lomdim everywhere
  • Build a home worthy of the collection
Cataloguing

Sefarim found nowhere else

A dedicated cataloguing team is documenting thousands of Sefarim and cross-referencing them with the major databases:

Otzar HaChochmaHebrewBooksThe Chabad Library

Already, dozens of Sefarim in the collection have been identified that are not available on any of these platforms — underscoring the collection’s unique value to Torah scholarship.
